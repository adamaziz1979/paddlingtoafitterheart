What is VO2 Max?

VO2 Max is the most oxygen your body can use during hard exercise, in millilitres per kilo of body weight per minute. It is one of the best single measures of heart and lung fitness. Higher is fitter.

Apple Watch estimates it from heart rate and pace on outdoor walks, runs and hikes. The Health app rates it against people of the same age and sex: Low, Below average, Above average or High. The coloured band is Above average for each age group.
